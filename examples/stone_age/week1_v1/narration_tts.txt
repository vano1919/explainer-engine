And grinding it takes even LONGER than cutting it. By the end of the week, a third of the colony does nothing but grain. [amused] Nansen runs the sum again. Hoping it changes. It doesn't. [intrigued] And cutting grain needs sharp STONE. So who knows stone?
[warmly] Nobody, except Darwin. He started out as a geologist, and he spots white lumps in the limestone. Flint! Then comes the knapping. [amused] Newton hits a flint. The flint WINS. By day four, they have a heap of flakes, scrapers and crude axes. And a great many bandaged fingers. [intrigued] But the real leap is HAFTING. Einstein mixes pine resin with charcoal, and it turns into glue. Then wet sinew is wrapped around the joint. As it dries, it SHRINKS, and the head locks tight. [softly] Oh, that's clever. [intrigued] Now they have axes. Which means somebody can finally BUILD something.
[warmly] Newton and Franklin design only what wood and stone allow. Levers. Rollers. A sledge. A grooved pulley that turns a pull the other way. [amused] Tesla sketches something much grander. Curie looks at it. Then at the pile of logs. Then at him. [softly] The problem? There is no metal. Not for a pin, not for an axle. Only WOOD. [suspenseful] They try anyway. Eight people on a rope, a log on the pulley, everybody pulling together. SNAP. [short pause] The wooden pin breaks. The log rolls. Everybody DIVES. [deadpan] Franklin is, technically, still alive. [thoughtful] Design is easy. Materials are HARD. [slowly] And tonight there's an even bigger problem. The water is making people SICK.
[warmly] John Snow has one rule: boil it. [deadpan] But there's no pot. So they dig a pit, line it with hide, and drop in stones that have sat in the fire. [softly] Hiss. Steam. It works. [suspenseful] Until a stone EXPLODES. Wet river cobbles hold water inside. Heat them, and the rock bursts. [amused] Shrapnel. Several small cuts. Everybody learns something, mostly about shrapnel. [warmly] And that's day seven. A secure camp. A stock of stone tools. A water routine. [slowly] Two people are sick. One cut is infected. One wrist is broken. [short pause] They're alive.
