# Video-Quality Root-Cause Audit (`VIDEO_QUALITY_ROOT_CAUSE_AUDIT.md`)

> **Document Purpose:** Deep structural root-cause analysis of why actual rendered Explainer Engine videos (`ep_toba` pilots and `ep_truce_1914` 84-shot full-film slices) fell short of production quality, separating cosmetic symptoms from underlying architectural causes across all 18 evaluation dimensions.  
> **Primary Empirical Evidence:** `episodes/ep_truce_1914/planning/full_film_2026-11-15/CORRECTIONS_LOG.md`, `SHOT_PLAN.md`, `mac_content_audit_classification.json` (43 audited defects), `docs/VIDEO_LANE.md`, `docs/wip/argon_2026-11-14/24_OPUS_VISUAL_CHECK_MAPS_S8.md`, `37_SLICE_REVIEW_1.md` (`V1–V6`), and `INCIDENT_JOURNAL_argon_loop.md`.

## 1. Executive Diagnosis: Why the Rendered Videos Were Not Good Enough

The quality failures observed in `ep_toba` and `ep_truce_1914` were **not** caused by a lack of rules in Markdown documents (the repository already has `200+` `BP-*`, `UR-*`, `VL-*`, `GC-*`, and `SC-*` rules). They were caused by **six systemic architectural disconnects** between what the planning schemas express and what the pixel/audio generators actually execute:

1. **Resolution & Texture Collapse Across the `60% video` <-> `30% still_25d` Seam:** Gemini keyframes are promoted to `2752x1536` (`2K+`) painterly masters, but `LTX-2.5 distilled` (`modal_ltx_jobs.py`) renders `video` shots at **`1280x704` (`0.9 MP`)** and Smooths out handmade brushstrokes into soft diffusion textures — without a spatial latent upscaler (`ltx-2.5-latent-spatial-upscaler-x2`) or a unified post-render grain/LUT pass to weld the two modalities together.
2. **Single-Frame (`Frame-0 Only`) Conditioning in the LTX Video Worker:** `ltx_pipelines.distilled` in `modal_ltx_jobs.py` only accepts `--image path 0 1.0` (no end-frame lock, no extend, no retake, no depth/pose control). Consequently, over a 4–8 second clip, characters drift off-model, fog blooms over candles (`S03_SH007`), and `continuation` chains degrade across consecutive tail-frame hand-overs.
3. **Uncontrolled Native LTX Speech & Audio Bleed:** Relying on LTX-2.5 to generate spoken dialogue directly inside the video diffusion pass caused `~0.75s` speech onset lag (`VL-02`), stuttered/duplicated words (`S06_SH008` saying `"Maybe."` twice), hallucinated words in silent beats (`S03_SH007` transcribing background noise as `"Oh my God"`), and weak lip-sync in close-ups (`docs/VIDEO_LANE.md`).
4. **The Un-Wired `directed25d.py` Gap & Blind Procedural FX Placement:** `episode/run.py` still runs the older plane-sliced `layers25d.py` (which creates Telea inpainting seams on camera moves), while `directed25d.py` remains an un-wired prototype. Worse, procedural weather/atmosphere effects (`snow`, `steam`, `breath`, `fire`) were applied without depth-occlusion masks against foreground faces/hands or frame-0 fade-in ramps (`37_SLICE_REVIEW_1.md` `V1–V6`).
5. **Stateless Prompt Compilation Causing Prop/Time-of-Day Drift (`43` Audited Defects in `ep_truce_1914`):** `mac_content_audit_classification.json` proved that shot prompts were generated without a stateful **Scene & Character State Machine** (e.g., Thomas wearing fingerless gloves in `S02_SH005` at `00:55` and bare hands in `S02_SH006` at `01:01`; `S01_SH005` rendering deep-blue night during a Christmas-morning dawn beat; `S06_SH009` hallucinating a rag on a pole).
6. **Synthetic Unit Tests Passing While Visual/Audio Reality Failed:** `4,191` unit tests passed on mocked `numpy` arrays while real rendered frames had clipped map HUD cards (`24_OPUS_VISUAL_CHECK_MAPS_S8.md`), uniform dot snow (`V1`), cropped boots/braziers (`V5`), and cut-off singing words (`S03_SH003` `'Nacht'`).

## 1. Strict Five-Layer Architectural Taxonomy (Do Not Force Everything Into a Skill)

A core flaw of ad-hoc agent setups is treating instructions, executable scripts, external daemons, and reasoning loops as interchangeable "skills." Explainer Engine enforces a strict 5-layer taxonomy:

## 3. End-to-End Capability Discovery, Selection, Execution, Validation & Failure Recovery Loop

When the system executes a production task or encounters a capability gap, it follows a deterministic **9-step closed loop**:

1. **Understand the Production Task:** Read `episodes/<id>/episode.json` (`look_id: crossworld_main_style_v1`, `era_line`) and `story/production_script.json` (`v4.0.0`) to identify the target stage (`G1..G10`) and shot route (`map_action`, `still_25d`, `video`).
2. **Identify Required Capabilities:** Map the task to required Contracts (`episode_timing/2`, `continuity_card/1`, `turn_pack/1`, `ltx_scene_test/3`), Tools (`explainer_engine/**`), and Skills (Classes 2–4).
3. **Check Internal Registry First (Case A before Case B):** Query the 5-table Registry (`Marketplaces`, `Plugins`, `Skills`, `MCP Servers`, `Executable Tools`). If a validated internal Tool + Skill exists, use it; only trigger external discovery (`skill-and-tool-discovery`) when a verified capability gap or repeated QA failure exists.
4. **Evaluate Suitability (10-Dimension Content Inspection & 100-Point Rubric):** Inspect actual code (`SKILL.md`, `scripts/*.py`, `bin/`, `.claude-plugin/plugin.json`, `hooks`, `monitors`, `mods`, `lspServers`, open & closed GitHub issues) and score against the 11-criterion rubric (with hard veto gates on permissive license, `SP-1..SP-6` security, and `$0.00` local validation cost).
5. **Select & Adapt to Explainer Engine Contracts:** Strip any external style defaults that conflict with `crossworld_main_style_v1` or `no_names`, and bind inputs/outputs to `audio/timing.json` (`ET.load_clock()`) and `explainer_engine/core/contracts.py`.
6. **Execute Through Governed Agents & Fail-Closed Hooks:** Run inside the stage-locked workflow (`stage_guard.py`, `episode/run.py`) protected by `.claude/settings.json` (`Bash|Monitor` and `mcp__.*__.*` with **fail-closed** `exit 2` / `onFailure: "block"` on security and spend).
7. **Validate Result (`VP-1..VP-6` & `AG-01..AG-08`):** Run unit/contract tests (`$0.00`), automated pixel/audio gates (`master_qa.py`, `cuts.py`, `VMAF`/`CAMBI`, `pyloudnorm`, Whisper transcript diff), and mandatory multi-frame contact-sheet (`look.py`) + real WAV playback checks (`AG-06`, `AG-07`).
8. **Record Evidence & Provenance:** Write stage records (`production/stages/<stage>.json` with input SHA-256 fingerprints and `clock_sha256`) and pin external Skill folders using the full 6-rule [`vercel-labs/skills/blob/main/src/skill-hash.ts`](https://github.com/vercel-labs/skills/blob/main/src/skill-hash.ts) `computedHash` (excluding `metadata.json`, `.git`, `__pycache__`, `__pypackages__`, rejecting external symlinks, sorting by UTF-8 bytes, normalizing `CRLF -> LF`).
9. **Diagnose & Recommend Improvements on Failure (`Class A / B / C` Repair Routing):**
   - **Class A Failure (Deterministic / Free Auto-Repair):** Boundary trim error, repeated LTX word (`S06_SH008`), unmuted silent-clip noise (`S03_SH007`), or loudness drift -> auto-update `edit_points.json` / re-mix stems (`$0.00`).
   - **Class B Failure (Plan / Parameter Repair):** Clipping of boots/HUD (`V5`), snow on face (`V3`), or LAB color mismatch (`S07_SH003`) -> adjust `direction.py` bbox margin, `living_fx.py` mask, or Reinhard LAB grade and re-render locally (`$0.00`).
   - **Class C Failure (Source Asset Defect):** Gloved vs bare hands (`S02_SH005`) or wrong time of day (`S01_SH005`) -> log in `remake_list` (`turn_pack.py`) or `scene_run.py` for Owner-approved regeneration.
