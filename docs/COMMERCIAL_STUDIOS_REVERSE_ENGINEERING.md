# Commercial AI Filmmaking Studios — Reverse-Engineered Production Architecture

## Purpose
Expensive paid story-to-video platforms (`$50–$250/month`: **LTX Studio**, **Higgsfield Popcorn / Cinema Studio / Short Drama**, **Google Flow**, **Runway Studio / Agent**, **Katalist.ai**, **InVideo AI**, **Animaps.ai**) do not rely on a single magical text-to-video prompt. Every one of them solves multi-angle environment consistency, acting, and cinematic continuity through a **6-layer software orchestration architecture** wrapped around image + video foundation models.

This document maps each commercial studio's internal pipeline directly to our open, zero-subscription engine (`Chat generate_image` [Nano Banana, `$0.00`] + `LTX-2.5` on Modal H100 + `Kokoro-ONNX` + `Headless Chrome WebGL2`).
