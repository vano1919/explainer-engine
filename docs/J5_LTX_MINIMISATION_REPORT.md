### Deterministic Decision Rule (`j5_limits.route_action_shot(shot)`)
Given a shot specification `shot`, a script applies `route_action_shot(shot)` in this exact order:
1. **If `lane == "HELPER"` or `subject in ('map', 'title', 'end')`** $
ightarrow$ `HELPER` (`0.0 s` LTX; Python 2D vector graphic).
2. **Else if `dialogue == True` or `scene == "tent"` speaker/reaction** $
ightarrow$ `J5_DIALOGUE_CARD` (`0.0 s` LTX; Blender 2.5D hero cards + `D-119` 4x2 mouth-variant swap + 2-layer robe/staff depth relief).
3. **Else if `requires_contact == True` or `"duel" in action_kind`** $
ightarrow$ `HYBRID_J5_HINGE_PLUS_LTX_IMPACT` (caps LTX at `min(seconds, 3.04 s)` = `73 frames` for the physical strike/parry, and pairs it with `seconds - 3.04 s` of free `J5_HINGED_RIG` blade-lock tension + spark particles).
4. **Else if `subject in ('column', 'armies', 'crowd')` (legs occluded by distance/mud/water/grass)** $
ightarrow$ `J5_BILLBOARD_CROWD` (`0.0 s` LTX; `>= 3` depth-sliced billboard planes with phase-staggered stride bob `1.6–1.9 Hz`, lateral parallax speed ratio `>= 1.45x`, and animated rain/ford-splash masks).
5. **Else if `hinged_prop` is set or `action_kind in ('drummer', 'commander_leads')`** $
ightarrow$ `J5_HINGED_RIG` (`0.0 s` LTX; hinged `<= 22°` upper-arm/drumstick/cloak/banner cycle + depth-relief camera push-in).
6. **Else** $
ightarrow$ `J5_RELIEF_CAM` (`0.0 s` LTX; Depth-Anything-V2-Small relief mesh camera `parallax <= 0.08` + local fire/smoke/rain ambience).

## 6. Claims in `docs/J5_DIALOGUE_COLLAGE.md` and `BP-176..BP-181` Found Wrong or Weak (With Sources & Quotes)

1. **Contradiction Between `BP-176` (`punch_scale_min = 1.3`) and `BP-178` (`punch_scale_min = 1.25`, `punch_scale_max = 2.0`):**
   - **What the repo claimed:** On `claude/project-thread-4nebmi`, `BP-176` set `punch_scale_min: 1.3`, while `BP-178` set `punch_scale_min: 1.25` and `punch_scale_max: 2.0`.
   - **Why it is wrong/weak:** First, a `1.27x` axial punch-in passes `BP-178` (`>= 1.25`) but violates `BP-176` (`< 1.30`). Second, [30-degree rule — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/30-degree_rule) defines the axial focal-length equivalent as the **"20 mm / 30 degree rule"** (going from a standard `35mm` medium shot to at least `50mm` = **`1.43x`**, or `50mm` to `70mm` = **`1.40x`**) and quotes Walter Murch (*In the Blink of an Eye*, p. 146):
     > *"[We] have difficulty accepting the kind of displacements that are neither subtle nor total: Cutting from a full-figure master shot, for instance, to a slightly tighter shot that frames the actors from the ankles up. The new shot in this case is different enough to signal that something has changed, but not different enough to make us re-evaluate its context."*
   - Third, `BP-178`'s `punch_scale_max: 2.0` contradicts `AC-13` (`ZOOM_MAX = 1.6` in `explainer_engine/compose/reuse.py`), where crops above `1.6x` go visibly soft (`j5_minute_v2`'s drummer cut-in `M2_drummer` was criticized as soft in `J5_ARGON_REVIEW.md` precisely because of over-cropping a 1080p still!). **Fix applied on our branch:** unified `punch_scale_min = 1.35` and `punch_scale_max = 1.6` across `BP-176` and `BP-178`.

2. **Weakness in `j5_cuts.py` (`SIZE_RANK` Allows `< 30°` Same-Subject Cuts Without Verifying Scale Ratio):**
   - **What the repo claimed:** `j5_cuts.py` defined `SIZE_RANK = {"wide": 0, "medium": 1, "close": 2}` and passed any cut where `dsize >= 1`, even if `dyaw == 0°` and the actual camera zoom difference between "wide" and "medium" cards was only `1.15x`.
   - **Source & Quote:** [Jump cut — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jump_cut) (citing Bordwell & Thompson, *Film Art: An Introduction*, 8th ed., p. 254):
     > *"Generally, if the camera position changes less than 30 degrees, the difference between the two shots is not substantial enough, and the viewer experiences the edit as a jump in the position of the subject rather than a change of point of view, which is jarring."*

3. **Over-Rigid `BP-177` (`require_establishing: true` Forces Shot 1 of Every Dialogue Scene to Be a Wide Two-Shot):**
   - **What the repo claimed:** `BP-177` states *"A valid plan requires: (a) Establishing wide shot at the start of the scene showing both speakers"*, and `j5_cuts.py` fails any scene whose first shot is not `size == "wide"`.
   - **Why it is wrong/weak:** Both classical continuity and montage theory frequently open a scene on a tight detail insert or speaker close-up ("in media res") and delay the wide establishing shot to Shot 2 or 3 (a **re-establishing shot**).
   - **Source & Quote:** [Continuity editing — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuity_editing):
     > *"The establishing shot is one that provides a view of all the space in which the action is occurring... The establishing shot can be used at any time as a reestablishing shot."*

4. **Internal Contradiction Between `BP-181` ("All Transitions Within a Scene Must Match") and `j5_minute.py` (`T1_tent_wide` Dissolve):**
   - **What the repo claimed:** `BP-181` (`J5_DIALOGUE_COLLAGE.md` §7i) states *"A scene must use a consistent transition family (all straight cuts or all matching dissolves)"*, yet `j5_minute.py` sets `cut="dissolve"` on `T1_tent_wide` (`scene="tent"`) while `T2..T6` (`scene="tent"`) use straight cuts!
   - **Why it is wrong/weak:** A transition on the *first* shot of a scene (`T1_tent_wide`) is an **inter-scene** transition (`march -> tent`), not an **intra-scene** transition. Furthermore, [Dissolve (filmmaking) — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissolve_(filmmaking)) (citing Cutting, Brunick & DeLong 2011) notes:
     > *"A dissolve overlaps two shots for the duration of the effect, usually at the end of one scene and the beginning of the next... Dissolves are usually kept to a minimum in most films and shows. Due mainly to stylistic tastes, it is very rare to see a shot which both begins and ends with a dissolve."*
   - **Fix applied in `j5_montage.py`:** `BP-181` now checks uniformity strictly across *intra-scene* cuts (`plan[idx - 1]["scene"] == s["scene"]`), allowing a scene-entry dissolve on Shot 1 followed by straight cuts inside the scene.

5. **Inaccurate Anatomical Claim in `docs/J5_DIALOGUE_COLLAGE.md` §7e ("Drawn 2D Characters Do Not Move the Chin That Way"):**
   - **What the repo claimed:** §7e states *"Drawn 2D characters do not move the chin that way: the mouth shape changes in place and the head nods a little."*
   - **Why it is weak:** In both traditional and limited 2D animation ([Limited animation — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limited_animation) and [DanielSWolf/rhubarb-lip-sync](https://github.com/danielswolf/rhubarb-lip-sync#mouth-shapes)), open-vowel visemes (`C`, `D`, `E`, `F`) *do* drop the lower jaw and chin contour. The bug in step 4c was not that 2D chins stay fixed, but that step 4c applied a **rectangular interior UV warp** that slid the beard texture downward while leaving the outer jaw/neck silhouette frozen ("like a mask"). Replacing the UV warp with `D-119` (`j5_variant_sheet.py` 4x2 cell replacement that includes the chin/beard contour inside the `512x512` head cell) fixes the artifact properly.
