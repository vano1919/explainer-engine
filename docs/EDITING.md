# Editing rules (ED-xx)

Creator notes of 2026-10-17 after the first full cut of Episode 1: audible jumps between read chunks (worst in Day 1), a zoom
that snaps back to the default view on the same picture, two music tracks plus the tick plus a bed all under the voice, and a
request for a faster final cut and a more mysterious feel at no extra cost. How to apply them: skill `editing-style`.

| ID | Rule | Enforced by |
|---|---|---|
| ED-01 | **Chunks join without a seam.** Every read chunk is levelled to the same gated loudness (spread of chunk loudness at most 1.5 dB) before the join, and each chunk edge gets a 30 ms fade inside the silence, so the join adds no click and moves no word. | [code] `audio/seams.py`, `production words` report `seams` |
| ED-02 | **A zoom never snaps back.** When consecutive shots show the same picture, the next shot starts exactly where the previous one ended (zoom and horizontal offset). The zoom may keep growing up to 1.25; past that the next shot eases out, never jumps. A different picture starts at its own default view, on a cut or a dissolve. | [code] `production/continuity.py`, check ZOOM-1 in `check_timed` |
| ED-08 | **The camera is at rest at every cut.** A move that ends at a cut to another picture slows to zero speed at its end, and the next picture's move starts from zero speed; inside a run of one picture the move runs on at constant speed through the cut (ED-02). So no cut ever lands in the middle of a zoom. | [code] `production/continuity.warp` |
| ED-12 | **No word is ever cut.** A pause is inserted only in the quietest 10 ms within 0.1 s of the measured gap (at most -40 dB, else it is skipped), entered and left on a 10 ms fade. After every read the dropout scan runs on the narration: no silence run may have speech level on either side (REVIEW from the first, FAIL from three). | [code] `audio/pause_pad.py`, `audio/dropouts.py` |
| ED-13 | **A long render survives a restart of the machine.** The final video is written in chunks of about 60 s, each a finished file (`chunks/c_NNNN.mp4`, written under a temporary name and renamed when whole); chunk edges are cuts, never inside a dissolve. Running the same command again reuses the chunks that are whole (a plan stamp of the timing, size and picture files protects against reusing stale ones) and only then joins them without re-encoding; a stale lock whose pid now belongs to another program is ignored. A restart loses at most one chunk. | [code] `production/assemble.py` (`chunk_bounds`, `mux`, `lock_alive`), test `test_chunked_render` |
| ED-03 | **One music at a time.** Music tracks never overlap except in a crossfade of at most 3 s (the earlier track fades out while the next fades in). The tick (`clockwork_loop`) is music and obeys this too; it sits at least 6 dB under every other music track and, as music, at least [BP-52](SOURCED_RULES.md)'s music distance under the voice (LU on the stems; its pre-mix gain cap is PV-29's `TICK_MAX_DB`). The layers at any moment stay within [BP-56](SOURCED_RULES.md) (AL-01); music fades in and out by [BP-62](SOURCED_RULES.md). | [code] PV-29, `timing` crossfade extension |
| ED-04 | **Few transition types, one look.** Hard cut is the default. A 1 s dissolve (UR-14) opens every period after the first; the episode opens with a 1 s fade from black and closes with a 1.5 s fade to black. No other transition type (no wipes, spins, flashes), except the motivated whip and flash entries at a story turn that MO-04 allows (D-48, [STORY_ENGINE](STORY_ENGINE.md)). One constant film look over the whole episode (slight vignette, fine grain), never switched on and off. | [code] `assemble` (`transition` per shot, `look`) |
| ED-05 | **Pace by content.** Dangerous or uncertain beats cut faster (3-5 s); wonder and big reveals hold longer (7-9 s); a joke lands on a cut right after its last word; a period boundary breathes (dissolve plus half a second of silence). Cuts stay inside punctuated pauses (UR-12). | [editorial] skill `editing-style`; UR-12, UR-31 code |
| ED-06 | **Speed is a finishing step.** A faster final cut is made from the finished video by time-compressing picture and sound together (pitch kept), never by re-synthesising the voice. The creator is shown the rates of [BP-02](SOURCED_RULES.md) (never above its ceiling) on the same two excerpts and picks one; only then is the final file made. The sped file is the delivered file, so its sound is [BP-63](SOURCED_RULES.md)'s codec (`assemble.AUDIO_CODEC`). Shorts keep their own tempo (SH rules). | [code] `finish/speed.py` |
| ED-07 | **Look and listen before reporting.** After a change to the cut, open frames on both sides of three joins of the same picture and three picture changes, and listen to the three loudest chunk joins; write what was seen and heard. | [editorial] OR-07 |

| ED-09 | **A period opens on a card.** Every period gets a 3-4 s title card on one of its own pictures (dark vignette, the period name large - "WEEK 1" - and a short line under it), a row of dots showing where we are in the timeline (Day 1 to Year 100, the current dot lit), the narrator's last word of the previous period still ringing over it (J-cut), the ticking dropped for the card. The card is drawn by our code from the script (`periods`), costs nothing, and the same layout is used in every period. | [code] `production/cards.py` (to be built), PV-30 |
| ED-10 | **Show the arithmetic.** Every period has at least one data visual that lets the viewer see the calculation behind the story: a counter, a bar or line chart, a before/after pair, a timeline, a ledger. Every number on it comes from the claim map (no invented number, PV-04 style check), is built in 0.6-1.0 s with a pop, stays at least 3 s and at most 7 s, and is drawn in the world's hand-drawn style, never a stock chart template. One idea per visual, at most 6 marks, labels at least 4% of the frame height. A visual replaces a picture for its duration or sits on a held picture; it never covers a speaking hero's face. | [code] `production/graphics.py` (to be built), PV-31; [editorial] skill `data-visuals` section of `editing-style` |
| ED-11 | **Priority order.** (1) the voice and what it says, (2) the cut that serves it (ED-01..ED-08), (3) the arithmetic and the cards (ED-09, ED-10), (4) pictures, (5) music, effects, texture. A bonus is dropped before a higher item suffers: no effect is kept that covers the voice or slows the cut. | [editorial] |
| ED-14 | **Dialogue coverage grammar** (D-58, the 2026-10-19 rewrite; sources per number: [STORYTELLING_EDITING_2026-10-19](research/STORYTELLING_EDITING_2026-10-19.md)). A scene opens on an establishing or two-shot of 2-4 s so the viewer knows where everyone stands; the LISTENER's reaction is shown on every 2nd-4th line and always on news, an insult or a revelation (Kuleshov); never more than 4 strict A/B alternations without a pattern break (a two-shot, an insert, a move). J/L cuts: on about a third of a scene's cuts the audio leads or trails the picture by 0.5-2 s, so the sound stitches the cut. Hold bands as hypotheses: dialogue 3-6 s, action 1.5-3 s, contemplative 6-12 s (MO-07 checks the variance). At most 14 never-before-seen framings a minute — a reused framing is free, and it is also the image budget. One music track per story chapter (2-4 changes per 10 min), changed only when the emotion changes, with ONE planned full dropout of 0.5-2 s before the biggest reveal, broken by an intentional sound. | [editorial] skill `dialogue-scenes`, agent `edit-planner`; variance in code as MO-07 |

**Not allowed without the creator's yes:** a paid effect pack, a stock clip, a new music track, any step that costs money.
Every effect above is made by our own code at no cost.
