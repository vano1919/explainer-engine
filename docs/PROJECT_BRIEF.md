# Project brief

## Mission
A sustainable, solo-operated, faceless YouTube channel with a recognisable identity: original 2D cartoon/vector
explainers, natural conversational narration, storytelling, purposeful movement and visual humour; plus a
semi-automated, resumable, QA-gated production pipeline. Entertaining and intelligent, not a dry lecture, a generic
AI slideshow or an over-edited template. Ranking videos are optional and never forced on a topic.

## Hard constraints (the creator's; precedence level 2)
- Hardware baseline: Windows, Intel i5-8400, AMD RX 5700 8 GB, 16 GB RAM, limited free disk. No upgrade assumed; no
  GPU acceleration assumed; no two large local models resident together (D-11).
- Budgets are ceilings (config/spend_caps.json, D-56); vidIQ <= 200 credits; no subscriptions without a clear need
  (ledger: [COST_AND_TIME_MODEL](COST_AND_TIME_MODEL.md)).
- No automatic public upload; the creator approves every publication (D-16 policy checks included).
- Original characters and visuals only: no third-party IP, footage or protected styles as the core identity.
- Facts must be sourced and checked; sensitive areas (politics, history, finance, health) get stricter review.

## Positioning (D-25, D-09)
**"Worlds collide"**: what happens when one world meets another. Only public-domain or factual worlds: historical eras,
prehistory, nature, science, myths, everyday technology and money. Examples: how long you would last as a medieval
peasant; what a Roman soldier's pay would buy; what a Victorian engineer would make of a phone; what if dinosaurs never
went extinct. Each video explains the real history or science behind the collision, with sources, and labels what is
speculation. English, general audience (teens and adults), set "not made for kids" (D-17). Fictional worlds that
belong to others (books, film, TV, games) appear only as **crossover analysis episodes** under the rights rules
([RIGHTS_AND_FAIR_USE](RIGHTS_AND_FAIR_USE.md), D-26), researched from what their fans discuss. Allowed formats: [SCRIPTING_SPEC](SCRIPTING_SPEC.md).
Visual direction: hand-drawn / doodle (D-04, being explored); the earlier box mascot is retired.

## Products
- Long-form: 5-11 minutes (target 7), the core product.
- Shorts: two or three standalone Shorts per long-form, each a separate script from the same verified dossier,
  recomposed for 9:16 (D-05).
- Per video package: dossier + claim map, script, storyboard, assets manifest, narration + timings, captions, audio
  events and licence records, thumbnail, titles, description, chapters, metadata, QA report, review bundle, final manifest.

## Success for the first cycle (not performance promises)
The prototype passes its acceptance criteria ([IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN](IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md), Phase 6); then three
long-forms and 6-9 Shorts are produced with logged hours and cost, and compared like with like
([PUBLISHING_AND_ANALYTICS](PUBLISHING_AND_ANALYTICS.md)). No virality, CTR, retention, income or monetisation
approval is promised anywhere in this project.

## Out of scope
Auto-upload; geopolitical satire; kids' content; personal-finance advice; copying any competitor's titles, scripts,
thumbnails or style; multi-channel "factory" cloning (SE-22); realistic synthetic people or events.
