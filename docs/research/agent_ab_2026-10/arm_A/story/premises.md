Winner: 1 (29). It has a body-felt stake (cold, hunger, a column with no food), two men who want opposite things, a
parley that can be staged in one tent, and every spoken fact has a claim id.
Why the others lost: 2 narrows the cast but loses the khan-vs-emperor clash; 3 reverses the fog and needs facts we do not
have; 4 and 5 are mechanics without a person; 6 has no antagonist on screen; 7 and 11 need claims we lack (honesty 2);
8 hides the khan, which the premise sells; 9 became the parley's turn inside 1; 10 drops the Napoleon hook; 12 has no
stakes in the body.
