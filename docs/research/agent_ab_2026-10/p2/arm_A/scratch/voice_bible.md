# Voice bibles (DW-11 extras that the cast schema has no field for)

napoleon: attitude to Murat: indulgent and exasperated, the cavalry is a weapon he cannot leave idle and cannot trust; to Kerim: uses him like a tool until the tool says something true; to the khan: curiosity he will not show. Never admits: that he needs the enemy to stand and fight because he has no other idea.
murat: attitude to Napoleon: adoring and impatient, wants to be sent; to Kerim: a useful horseman, not a man to be listened to. Never admits: that his horses are dying and he knows it.
genghis: attitude to Kerim: interest in a man who answers straight; to Napoleon: the patience of a hunter looking at a strange animal. Never admits: that he wants the guns more than the passage he is selling.
kerim: attitude to Napoleon: awe and fear of the emperor's silence; to Murat: contempt for a man who kills horses; to the khan: a recognition he does not want. Never admits: that he boasted of the guns to be taken seriously for once.
