# Premises (stage 1, story-architect) -- episode "What if Napoleon marched into Genghis Khan?"

Scores 1-5: surprise / body stakes (cold, hunger, shame, losing someone) / character conflict / visual drama / honesty (can the claim map carry it) / rights safety.

Winner: 1, taking the supply war of 4 as its middle, the interpreter of 3 as its hinge and the job offer of 5 as the parley's temptation.
Why the others lost: 2 is one scene, not an episode; 3 is a good hinge but has no big stakes alone; 4 lacks a face for the enemy; 5 has no body stakes;
6 is a range table, not a story; 7 leaves the 1812 army out; 8 has no conflict between heroes; 9 asks for more than the claim map holds; 10 has no
conflict of wills; 11 cannot be carried by the claims (and loses the intimate scenes); 12 has no conflict at all.
