# Voice bibles (stage 1; words are for writers, never quotes). Narrator: not a cast member; dry, quick storyteller (voice-caster picks).
Rule for all four: a real person speaks only lines that fit the documented record of who they were (claim ids); the invented hero is C-9x story.

## napoleon (real, basis public; claims C-04, C-05)
- Register: clipped orders and short questions that are really commands; counts things (guns, paces, hours); the artillery officer's eye.
- Sentence length: 4-12 words; one longer turn only at the peak (central scene).
- Signature moves (3): (1) turns a feeling into a number or a distance; (2) answers a plea with a new question; (3) goes silent and looks at the ground before deciding.
- Favourite words: ground, range, hour, decide, count, guns. Forbidden: modern slang, "okay", self-praise adjectives, any famous catchphrase.
- attitude_to_each_other_character: murat = a brilliant tool he must hold back, indulgent; tahir = a useful rider, then a man he owes; genghis = the first enemy who will not stand still, so he studies him.
- what_they_never_admit: that he is afraid there is no battle that can finish this enemy; that he needs one more win more than he needs the army.
- Traits as choices: prefers the offensive (orders the advance when told the enemy runs, C-04); drags the wagons rather than burn them (S06); gives away the guns he loves (S13).

## murat (real, basis public; claims C-13)
- Register: loud, quick, vain, fond of gesture; speaks in "charge" and "now"; jokes at his own cost without noticing.
- Sentence length: 3-9 words; exclamations; interrupts.
- Signature moves: (1) offers to charge before the question is finished; (2) blames the ground or the weather, never the horses; (3) a boast that is also a plea for permission.
- Favourite words: now, charge, glory, my cavalry, grass. Forbidden: figures, caution, "retreat".
- attitude: napoleon = the one man whose nod he wants; tahir = "my Mameluke", half pet, half scout; genghis = never speaks to him.
- what_they_never_admit: that the horses are finished and that he did not look at them.
- Traits as choices: gallops his men and horses to ruin (C-13) in S04-S05; his plumed grey horse is blowing before the first shot (P02); asks for the charge a third time in S10 and goes down with the horse.

## genghis (real, basis public; claims C-11, C-10)
- Register: slow, plain, few words, nothing wasted; speaks in horses, grass, distance and weather; asks a question once.
- Sentence length: 3-10 words; long pauses (the performer adds them).
- Signature moves: (1) feeds or gives something before he asks; (2) tests a man with an easy question whose honest answer costs; (3) answers a threat by naming the weather or the herd.
- Favourite words: horse, grass, ride, tomorrow, keep. Forbidden: boasting, titles for himself, cruelty described, modern words.
- attitude: napoleon = a gifted man who is too sure of one road; tahir = a man whose honest answer earns his life (the Jebe story, C-11).
- what_they_never_admit: that bows do not reach a gun and that he is counting on the lure, not on strength.
- Traits as choices: keeps the man who tells him the truth (spares Tahir); recruits skill from the enemy (asks for the guns, not the emperor); withdraws instead of fighting for ground.

## tahir (invented, basis personality; premise C-9x, facts C-14, C-15)
- Invented rider of the Mameluke company, born on the Crimean coast; speaks the Kipchak-family tongue of his grandmother, which is close to what the steppe riders speak (C-15).
- Register: dry, exact, a translator's habit of "He says" and "He does not say"; a rider's practical jokes when frightened.
- Sentence length: 4-12 words; shortens when scared.
- Signature moves: (1) corrects one word before translating the rest; (2) answers a threat with a practical detail (horses, water); (3) hesitates before the truth, then says it whole.
- Favourite words: exactly, he says, water, my grandmother, horses. Forbidden: grand speeches, flattery.
- attitude: napoleon = the Emperor he serves and slightly fears; murat = a man he must not contradict; genghis = a man he expected to hate and cannot.
- what_they_never_admit: that the steppe sounds like home and that he is tempted to stay.
- Traits as choices: counts the spare horses of the riders instead of the riders (S06); admits the guns' range to the khan when a lie would save him (S08); softens nothing when translating the emperor's "no" (S13).
