# Episode-first contract (v4.3, D-131): one folder, one id, one route per shot

Since the v4.3 rebuild (the creator, 2026-11-12; plan `PLAN_v4_3_IMPLEMENTATION.md` P1) an episode lives in ONE folder
`episodes/<episode_id>/`: `episode.json` (schema `episode_manifest`), `story/production_script.json` (schema
`production_script`, `schema_version` 4.0.0), its planning, assets, QA and edit. Code: `explainer_engine/episode/`
(`context.py` the folder and its era isolation, `validate.py` EP rules, `router.py` RT rules, `run.py` the one runner, RN rules). Dialogue inside scenes is
SC-01 of [STORY_ENGINE](STORY_ENGINE.md); picture making through the Gemini chat is GC-01..GC-17 of
[GEMINI_CONSISTENCY_RULES](GEMINI_CONSISTENCY_RULES.md).

| ID | Rule | Checked by | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| EP-01 | **One id per episode.** The folder is named by `episode.json`'s `episode_id`, and every JSON document in the folder that names an episode (`episode_id`, or `episode` as a plain string) names the same one (the Toba folder carried `ep_toba` and `ep_toba_74000bce_12min` side by side, plan F12). | `python -m explainer_engine.episode.validate episodes/<id>` | FAIL |
| EP-02 | **One look per episode.** `episode.json` names its `look_id`, its map system `map_style`, the intake floor `image_native_min_long_side` (the chat tool's 1376) and the working master `image_min_long_side` (2752 after the free 2x upscale, never under 2048, plan 0.7). The look's two sentences (`look.style_sentence`, `look.face_sentence`) are set in the reference round with the creator; until then picture export refuses to start. | `episode_manifest.schema.json`; `imagegen/turn_pack` (P3) | FAIL |
| EP-03 | **Gates measure or say NOT_RUN.** Every acceptance row (`qa/gates.row`) is PASS or FAIL from a measurement on what the run made, or NOT_RUN with its reason; a report with a NOT_RUN is `INCOMPLETE`, never a pass. The map count and the master's length come from the episode's own script (no 2-minute pilot constants, plan F2); a synthesized placeholder narration FAILs (F4); the visual mix is by duration (RT-04); the Wan hand-over (frame [-5]) is checked on the script's own chained shots. A gate audit FAILs only on a FAIL row; REVIEW rows (hypothesis rules, D-59) make it REVIEW; a gate row on a hypothesis or an engineering default misses as REVIEW (`level`), never FAIL. | `episode/acceptance.gates`, `orchestration/rule_enforcer.wan_chain_check`, `run_all_gate_audits` | FAIL |
| RT-01 | **Every shot has one route and a written reason** (D-131): `map_action` (Python map with an action layer, P4), `still_25d` (a picture moved by the layered 2.5D renderer with motion imitation, P5) or `wan` (Wan on Modal, P6). The router is deterministic; a hand-picked route sets `route_locked` and must carry its own `route_reason`. | `episode/router.route_script`; schema | FAIL |
| RT-02 | **Wan only where a body performs** what a still cannot fake (running, fighting, throwing, handling an object, a fall), and for the speaker's performance shot of a played scene (a Wan clip, no lip-sync, no talking still; Q4, Q7). Camera moves, weather, fire, water, crowds at a distance, establishing shots and objects at rest are `still_25d`. | `episode/router.route_shot` | — (the router decides) |
| RT-03 | **Geography and numbers are `map_action`**: map shots, spread, routes and counters are drawn by Python over a letter-free plate, each action tied to a spoken word (`actions[].at_word`, Q6). `actions` appear only on `map_action` shots and `motion_imitation` only on `still_25d` shots. The plate's relief is Natural Earth's shaded relief raster (never invented terrain, F6), in the episode's `map_look` (palette tokens), lettered in the map face (Inter); the action vocabulary (`route_arrow`, `front_line`, `spread`, `plume`, `pulse`, `highlight`, `label`, `counter`, `zoom`, `split_compare`) is anchored in lon/lat of `episode.json` `places`, and a shot whose actions name no place is a styled card (the other 10 % moments). An action whose word is not in the shot's line, in order, or whose place is unknown, stops the render. | schema (`ShotSpec.allOf`); `maps/actions.plan`; `maps/relief.raster` | FAIL |
| RT-04 | **The visual mix is measured by duration, never by shot counts** (R-C): `router.mix_report` sums the seconds per route from the timed spine (`basis: measured`) or, before the read exists, from `target_duration_seconds` (`basis: estimated`). The creator's target 10/30/60 (map/still/wan) is a guide, not a quota; the script decides. | `episode/router.mix_report` | REVIEW |
| RT-05 | **No unapproved engine in a shot**: `video_engine_tier` keeps only archive labels that name a route we run; the Gemini video engines (`GEMINI_LIGHT_VIDEO_I2V`, `GEMINI_VIDEO_API_BATCH`) were never approved and are refused. | schema | FAIL |
| RT-06 | **A `still_25d` shot is layered and alive** (plan P5): real depth (Depth-Anything V2 Small, free; the formula ramp only as a flagged fallback), split into depth planes with Telea fills under the nearer ones, each plane moved by one affine transform with parallax by its disparity (the near plane travels about 5 % of the width; BP-182 cap); a face lies in one plane (YuNet), so it is never warped. `motion_imitation` names one camera path at most (push, pull, truck, crane, orbit_small) plus handheld and rack_focus, and the effects of the light, atmosphere, surface (a region is required) and subject families; BP-185 asks for two families beyond the camera; a shake or a lightning strike lands only on an impact (`at_word`, `at_frame`, or a sound event's `picture_event`, BP-24, BP-26). Every frame is a pure function of its number. The clip report measures coverage, edge tears, the face's plane and landmarks, frozen runs (BP-174), flashes, the near plane's travel, the start frame, chunk equality and speed; a start/end pair with another layout is not cross-moved (two shots). | `production/layers25d.render_clip`; `episode/acceptance.gates` (camera_motion_measured) | FAIL (BP-185, frozen, coverage, faces), REVIEW (the rest) |
| RT-07 | **A Wan clip is measured, retried once, then replaced** (plan P6): one adapter (`aivideo/wan_adapter`) in front of every backend; a paid backend runs only with the creator's OR-23 approval and only when every attempt's estimate (from a measured speed, never a guess) fits under the approval's cap, else nothing is sent. Each clip is checked on what it is: it decodes, has its frames, is not a still (BP-174) and has no frozen run (BP-164), is not black, blown or flat, starts on its picture and ends on its end picture, and keeps the face of its picture (SFace). A FAIL is retried once with a new seed; a second FAIL falls back to `still_25d` with the shot's `fallback_motion_imitation` (only on a `wan` shot), and the report says so. Every attempt writes a ledger line (submit, done or error, sha256). A speaker's shot is `wan` and at most BP-108's dialogue length. Money goes through the run's `core/ledger.Run` (each paid attempt reserved before it is sent, the 80 % stop) under the creator's line in `config/spend_caps.json` (`modal`: no line = a zero approved budget, every paid call refused, D-131). The Modal backend launches the worker `training/pods/wan/modal_wan_jobs.py` (the v15 template: timeouts from the caps, retries 0, preflight before the GO gate, weights only from the Volume, inputs pinned by sha256, outputs to HF as they are made) only on rung L4 of the OR-37 ladder (`cloud/modal_run`: L0 local tests, L1 CPU imports, L2 CPU weight shas, L3 GPU smoke that measures the speed, L4 the shot), priced from `cloud/modal_schedule` and the L3 speed; a launch file with a null pin (model, revision, file shas, pipeline, settings, caps, OR-22) refuses. Test T4 (plan section 7) runs as L1, L2, L3 and then L4 on 2-3 shots, each rung on the creator's word where it bills. | `aivideo/wan_adapter.run_shot`, `qa_clip`, `ModalWanBackend`; `training/pods/wan/modal_wan_jobs.py`; `cloud/modal_run`; `episode/router.talking_check`; schema | FAIL (decode, frames, still, money), REVIEW (the rest) |
| SP-01 | **One sound plan per episode, whatever the picture route** (plan P7, R-D): `audio/sound_plan.json` lists per shot its events with their time (an authored `sound_events` item, else the spoken word that names it on the spine's or the estimated word clock, else the visible action at a fifth of the shot), per place one ambience bed with a J/L bridge of half a second each side of a place change, and per segment one music cue. Every event is a class of `EVENT_CLASSES` (the one list the library is filled from, once); an era refuses the classes it never heard (horses and swords in the Pleistocene) and a take whose tags place it in a later world (a garage, traffic). Sources are library first: a procedural numpy sound is a placeholder, a class without a take is missing, and both go on the plan's `needs` list. | `episode/sound_plan.build`, `resolve`, `needs`, `shippable` | FAIL (unknown class, a time after the shot's end) |
| SP-02 | **One renderer for the five stems, at one level everywhere** (plan P7, Q5, BP-186): `audio/stems.render` plays the plan into narration, music, ambience, sfx and mix at 48 kHz stereo: the narration is the episode's real read (no code can make a voice; the zip's formant voice and numpy cello are removed), every event of a kind at the same RMS, music and bed ducked under the voice (BP-53), the place's room tone and room. A shippable render refuses a placeholder, a missing class or a missing read; a preview may hold them and says it cannot ship (the acceptance row `sound_is_real` FAILs it). The background is measured per shot under speech and in the gaps and the route groups' medians are compared (BP-186). | `audio/stems.render`, `level_report`; `episode/acceptance.gates` (sound_is_real, equal_level_*) | FAIL (placeholder, missing, no read), REVIEW (BP-186) |
| RN-01 | **One runner, one record per stage** (plan P8): `python -m explainer_engine.episode run episodes/<id> --stage <stage>` (or `--to <stage>`; G1..G10 name their stage) runs the stages of plan section 4 in order: `script` (G1-G3), `sound_plan` (G4), `pictures` (G5), `voice` (G6), `clips` (G7), `sound` (G8), `assemble` (G9), `qa` (G10). Each writes `production/stages/<stage>.json` with its gate rows (EP-03), its verdict, what it made and the fingerprint of what it was made from (episode, script, its own input files, the record before it). | `episode/run.run_stage`, `run_to`, `state` | FAIL |
| RN-02 | **A stage starts only on a good, fresh record before it**: missing, stale (its inputs changed) or not PASS/REVIEW refuses the start. `--rehearsal` lets a stage start after a FAIL or an INCOMPLETE to rehearse the chain on what exists; every later record says `rehearsal`, the QA stage of a rehearsal FAILs `not_a_rehearsal`, and the manifest's `all_gates_passed` is false. `--to` keeps fresh records and runs stale ones again; `--force` runs them all. A contract a stage cannot get past is its FAIL row on record. | `episode/run.check_before` | FAIL |
| RN-03 | **The read times the shots** (G6): `audio/narration.wav` (the real read, the lines in the shots' order) and its Scribe transcript `audio/narration.scribe.json` are aligned to the lines (`audio/scribe_words.align`, REVIEW under its coverage); a cut between two spoken shots falls in the middle of the pause between their lines, shots without a line share their pause by their planned lengths (the line keeps 0.25 s of air, a house default), and the shots tile the read frame by frame. `audio/timing.json` holds the measured lengths and each shot's word spine; the script is not edited (`timing.timed_script` applies them to a copy for G7-G10). No read: FAIL, and a rehearsal uses the planned lengths. | `episode/timing.align_read`, `retime` | FAIL (no read), REVIEW (coverage) |
| RN-04 | **Clips by route, never paid by the runner** (G5, G7, D-131): a picture enters G7 only through the turn packs' intake and upscaled to its master (P3; otherwise FAIL rows, used only in a rehearsal); `map_action` shots are drawn by the action map (P4), `still_25d` by the layered renderer with the sound plan's picture events and the measured spine (P5); a `wan` shot takes the Wan adapter's QA'd clip (`production/wan/<shot>.json`, P6) and FAILs without one; a rehearsal draws it with the adapter's 2.5D fallback and says so. A clip whose shot, pictures, spine and size are unchanged is reused. Paid ledger lines (`logs/*ledger*.jsonl`) must each name an approved plan (`paid_calls_approved`, OR-23). | `episode/run.stage_pictures`, `stage_clips`, `paid_lines`; `episode/acceptance.gates` | FAIL |
| RN-05 | **Assembly in chunks** (G9, RULE-R1, F15): `production/assemble.assemble_shot_clips` streams the clips one shot at a time into chunks that start on a shot (`chunk_bounds`), blends the cut plan's transitions on the boundary frames (from the two clips only, so a chunk draws what one long render draws), reuses a chunk whose stamp (clips' sha256, cuts, size, code) matches, draws only `--chunks A:B` when asked, and muxes the mix in once every chunk exists. A clip shorter than its shot is refused (never stretched or repeated). | `production/assemble.assemble_shot_clips` | FAIL |
| RN-06 | **QA over what the run made** (G10): the acceptance report (EP-03) on the timed script, the shot records, the stems report, the stem audit and the master, the contact sheet from the master, `production/production_manifest.json` with every stage's state. The HF upload and the creator's approval follow outside the runner. | `episode/run.stage_qa` | FAIL |
