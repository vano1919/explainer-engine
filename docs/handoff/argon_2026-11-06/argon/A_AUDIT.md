## 3. Every Place Where the Plans Still Let the Video Be Fitted to the Pictures (Instead of Pictures to the Script)

1. **Pilot v2/v3 & P1 Stills-First Reverse Pipeline (`PILOT_1MIN_PLAN_v2.md`, `PILOT_1MIN_PLAN_v3.md`, `HANDOFF_P1.md`):**
   - **What the plan does:** Renders 7 room stills (`pilot_room`) and 4 battle stills in Blender *first*, without a locked script or recorded narration spine, and then writes motion prompts and cuts a 63-second pilot video around those 11 pre-existing stills.
   - **Proof from the files:** `HANDOFF_P1.md` ("Open points") explicitly states that P1 had to abandon `EXEC_QUEUE.md`'s intended script actions (*"elder walks, guest hands plate, candle unlit"*) and use the rules thread's static motion lines instead *because those objects/poses were not in the pre-rendered stills*. Furthermore, `PILOT_1MIN_PLAN_v3.md` notes that `battle_f000` *"has no commander and no river, so its card names no hero"*—fitting the shot card to what the Blender render happened to contain rather than putting the commander and river ford into Blender from the script!

2. **P1 (`p16`) Cost-Driven Clip Reuse Dictating Montage Structure (`HANDOFF_P1.md`):**
   - **What the plan does:** Forces `pilot_minute.py` to reuse the two 185-frame (`7.71 s`) clips from `p15` (`room_two_shot`, `battle_f000`) plus 8 fixed-frame LTX clips (`145f`, `121f`, `97f` = 1,088 frames) to hit ~63 seconds.
   - **Why it reverses the rule:** Clip durations (`7.71 s`, `6.04 s`, `5.04 s`, `4.04 s`) are chosen from pre-baked LTX frame counts (`185`, `145`, `121`, `97`) before any voice recording exists, forcing the edit/montage to fit fixed LTX clip lengths rather than cutting clips to `audio_spine/2` word timestamps.

3. **J5 Dialogue Step 1 Asset-Driven Camera & Shot Selection (`HANDOFF_STEP1.md`, `PLANS.md`, `MATERIAL.md`):**
   - **What the plan does:** Chose a 4-shot sequence (`S1` medium two-shot, `S2` OTS H2->H1, `S3` OTS H1->H2, `S4` CU H1) and waist-up framing based on the 4 turnaround sheets available in `assets_B_hero_sheets` (and because `B_H1` has no legs below the belt).
   - **Why it reverses the rule:** Available hero sheet angles dictated camera placement and framing rather than script cards dictating which hero sheet angles/poses must be generated.

4. **`PLANNING_CHAIN_PLAN.md` §2 & §3 — Defaulting Every Shot to an LTX Clip Without a "Needs LTX" Gate:**
   - **What the plan does:** Section 3 Rule 1 states *"1 shot = 1 clip by default, unless `duration_sec` exceeds 4.0 s"*, and the `visual_script/1` schema draft in Section 2 routes a 4.12 s dialogue shot with a static camera (`S03-02`: *"Mirian tilts head slightly, speaks; camera stays static"*) to `J4` LTX (`97 frames`).
   - **Why it reverses the rule:** It treats LTX video generation as the default for every shot instead of testing whether a free static/Python-moved still or J5 2.5D card shot suffices (Strategy Rule 1). Worse, splitting shots automatically at an arbitrary `4.0 s` LTX drift threshold creates unmotivated visual cuts driven by LTX's limits rather than the script's narrative beats.

5. **`PLANNING_CHAIN_PLAN.md` §4 vs. `REUSE.md` §2 — `R4` ("Clip Retime Reuse") and Loose "Asset Overlap" Matching:**
   - **What the plan does:** `PLANNING_CHAIN_PLAN.md` §4 defines `R4` as *"Clip Retime Reuse: Reusing an LTX clip with speed adjustment for background characters or loops"* and states that the solver *"checks `place_id`, camera angles, and asset overlap to output candidate reuse pairs"*.
   - **Why it reverses the rule:** Retiming or looping an old video clip to fill a new script beat is the textbook definition of fitting the video to old pictures/clips. Similarly, matching stills by partial *"asset overlap"* instead of exact `in_shot` equality (`Q3 A` and `REUSE.md` §2) injects leftover props or missing characters from earlier shots into new script beats.

6. **Legacy `explainer_engine/compose/reuse.py` (`setup_key`) Ignores Required Props and Character Poses:**
   - **What the code does:** `setup_key(shot)` returns `(shot["background"], shot.get("angle", "eye"), cast)`—ignoring non-cast props in `in_shot` and character poses/states (`pose`).
   - **Why it reverses the rule:** If Shot 8 has the same `(background, angle, cast)` as Shot 2, `compose/reuse.py` crops Shot 2 even if Shot 8's script requires the character to hold a scroll (`letter_scroll`) or stand instead of sit—forcing Shot 8's picture to show Shot 2's state.

7. **Blender Set Layouts (`world_layouts.json`, `pilot_stills.py`) — Pre-Furnished Sets Leaking Unlisted Props into Camera Frustums:**
   - **What the plan does:** Places 18+ Poly Haven furniture/prop pieces into a room (`PILOT_1MIN_PLAN_v2.md`: *"if a clock belongs in the room, build the room with the clock, the table and everything else"*) and renders wide/medium cameras across that room.
   - **Why it reverses the rule:** Whenever a camera frustum intersects background props not in the shot's `in_shot` list (e.g., `bookshelf`, `cabinet`, `jug`, `plates`, or the kneeling `elder` in `room_reverse`), Qwen paints them into the first frame and LTX animates or hallucinates around them. The V3 frustum check must actively hide or fail any 3D object in the camera frustum that is not in `in_shot`.

8. **`route_shots.json` ([`tests/fixtures/route_test/route_shots.json`](https://github.com/vano1919/explainer-engine/blob/argon/handoff-2026-11-06/tests/fixtures/route_test/route_shots.json)) — Over-Routing Static/Camera-Move Shots to J2/J4 LTX:**
   - **What the fixture does:** Even after reducing LTX clips from 18 to 9 (`43.2 s` across 24 shots, [`HANDOFF_route_script.md`](https://github.com/vano1919/explainer-engine/blob/argon/handoff-2026-11-06/docs/handoff/argon_2026-11-06/handoffs/HANDOFF_route_script.md)), the fixture still routes shots like `S05-04` (*"commander stares at the waves from a ship rail, wet wind... camera move: push_in"*, `3.0 s`, `73 frames`) and `S04-01` (marching column where a camera pan/push or 2.5D card parallax + Blender rain FX works) to paid `J2`/`J4` LTX.
   - **Why it reverses the rule:** A 3-second emotional close-up (`S05-04`) with a simple `push_in` and wind/hair secondary motion is handled for $0 by J5 (`assemble_25d` card push-in + procedural breath/blink/cloth sway), saving both LTX seconds and Qwen restyle risk.
