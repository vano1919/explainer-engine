## 1. English Disclosure Texts (`en`)

### YouTube Video Description (`en.video_description`)
> HISTORICAL DOCUDRAMA NOTE: This film is a painted historical docudrama about the Christmas Truce of December 1914 on the Western Front. All historical context, dates, locations (Flanders, Frelinghien-Houplines, Fleurbaix), Pope Benedict XV's failed 7 December 1914 truce appeal, Princess Mary's brass gift tins, the carols sung across the wire, the joint burials of the fallen, and the 26 December return to arms are grounded in documented historical records. To tell this story through human eyes, the three named soldiers—Private Thomas Hale, Gefreiter Friedrich Brandt, and Sergeant Albert Harris—are invented composite characters, and their personal letters, family photograph, and spoken dialogue are dramatized reconstructions inspired by surviving soldiers' letters and war diaries rather than verbatim historical quotes.
